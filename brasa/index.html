A family kitchen built around one oven

Marta and Luis Ferreira opened Brasa with a 3-ton oven they built themselves from Neapolitan brick. Everything on the menu passes through it or the grill beside it.

Dough rests for 48 hours. Produce comes from farms in Harvard and Lancaster when it's in season, and the chouriço still comes from the same Fall River butcher Luis's father used.
