# Brasa brand book

> Brasa is a fictional restaurant, created as a portfolio concept by Jean Berrios-Marquez.

## The brand in one line

**This site should feel like a warm, family-run kitchen to Worcester locals planning a night out, unlike the polished chain restaurants downtown.**

## Who we are

A family-owned, wood-fired kitchen on Shrewsbury Street, open since 2011. Marta and Luis Ferreira built the oven by hand. Everything on the menu passes through the oven or the grill beside it, and that oven is the brand.

**Personality:** warm, proud of the craft, neighborly, never fussy.
**Not:** trendy, upscale-stiff, cute, or "foodie."

## Voice

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Name the real thing: "Fall River chouriço," "hen of the woods" | "Mouth-watering," "delectable," "artisan" |
| Short, plain sentences, like a server talking at the table | Long adjective chains |
| Specifics: "48-hour dough," "last pizza out at 9:30" | Vague promises: "the best pizza in town" |
| Sentence case everywhere | ALL CAPS, exclamation points |

**Sample lines**
- Changes daily. Last pizza out at 9:30.
- Gluten-free dough available for any pizza (+3).
- Seats up to 28. Family-style menus start at $45 per person.

## Name and wordmark

- The wordmark is "Brasa" set in Young Serif, with no symbol. Never stretch it, outline it or add a tagline lockup.
- Use paper (#F3F1E6) on basil, or ink on sage/white. Never saffron.
- Leave at least the height of the "B" as clear space on all sides.

## Color

The full table is in [`design-system/README.md`](design-system/README.md#color).

- **Basil green** is the brand. Lead with it.
- **Saffron** is the fire. It appears only on buttons and the oven ember, so it always means "do this."
- **Wood brown** appears once per page, for private events.
- Rough proportion: 50% sage/white, 35% basil, 10% wood, 5% saffron.

## Typography

- **Young Serif** carries the warmth: the name, headings, prices and big numbers.
- **Work Sans** does the work: body text, dish names, buttons.
- Prices are plain numbers (16, not $16.00).

## Photography (for the real build)

- Shoot real food at the pass, under warm light, with fire and char visible.
- Show hands, the oven mouth and the dining room on a full night.
- Avoid stock photos, overhead "flat lay" spreads and empty-room shots.

## Signature element

**The oven arch.** The arch-topped card with the ember glow is Brasa's mark on the web. Use it once per page, for the most important information (tonight's menu, a seasonal special). Don't repeat it as decoration.

## Do's and don'ts

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Hours, address and phone within one scroll of the top | Hide hours in the footer only |
| Update "Tonight from the oven" often | Leave a stale special up for months |
| Keep the Google Business Profile hours and photos identical to the site | Let the two drift apart |
| Use the ghost button only on green | Put two saffron buttons side by side |
