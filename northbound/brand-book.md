# Northbound Roofing brand book

> Northbound Roofing is a fictional business, created as a portfolio concept by Jean Berrios-Marquez.

## The brand in one line

**This site should feel straight-talking and dependable to Central MA homeowners with a leak, unlike the storm-chasing roofers who disappear after the check clears.**

## Who we are

A family-owned roofing crew based in Shrewsbury since 2008. The owners inspect every roof themselves, send photos, and give a fixed price before any work starts. No subcontractors.

**Personality:** direct, competent, local, calm under pressure.
**Not:** salesy, flashy, "luxury," or scary ("Your roof could collapse!").

## Voice

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Lead with the outcome: "Leaks fixed. Roofs replaced." | "Premier exterior solutions provider" |
| Give numbers: "quote in 48 hours," "from $350" | "Affordable," "competitive pricing" |
| Explain like a neighbor: "It's usually the flashing, not the shingles." | Trade jargon without explanation |
| Say exactly what a button does: "Book a free inspection" | "Learn more," "Submit" |

**Sample lines**
- We'll call within one business day to set a time.
- We protect your landscaping, then sweep the yard for nails with a magnet.
- Water coming in right now?

## Logo

- The mark is a copper roof chevron over a slate house outline, followed by "Northbound Roofing" in Archivo 800 at 78% width.
- On dark backgrounds, use white for the house outline and keep the chevron copper.
- Minimum size is 24px for the mark and 140px for the full lockup.

## Color

The full table is in [`design-system/README.md`](design-system/README.md#color).

- **Slate** is the steady base. **Copper** (a nod to copper flashing) means "act now."
- The phone number is always copper and always a tap-to-call link.
- Rough proportion: 60% white/mist, 30% slate, 10% copper.

## Typography

- **Archivo condensed, extra bold** for headlines and the phone number. It's sturdy, like work-truck lettering.
- **Public Sans** for everything else. It's clear and readable on a phone in a driveway.

## Photography (for the real build)

- Real crews on real local roofs, plus before/after pairs from the same angle.
- Close-ups of problems found: rotted decking, failed flashing. This builds trust.
- Avoid stock photos of smiling handshakes, drone shots of mansions, and AI-generated houses.

## Signature element

**The copper-topped quote form.** It sits in the hero on the homepage and on every service page. It's the one place the brand uses a shadow.

## Do's and don'ts

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Keep the phone number visible on every screen size | Hide the number behind a menu on mobile |
| List real towns with honest response times | Claim "serving all of New England" |
| Show starting prices | Make every price "call for quote" |
| Use "Step N" only for real sequences | Number things that aren't steps |
| Match name, phone and hours to the Google Business Profile exactly | Use a different phone number on Google |
