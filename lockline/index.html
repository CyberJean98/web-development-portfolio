What we check

These are the five places small businesses get hurt most often. We check all of them without installing anything on your computers.

Email and logins

Two-factor coverage, reused passwords found in public breach data, and whether your domain can be spoofed in phishing emails.

Your website

Outdated WordPress core, themes and plugins, exposed admin pages, missing HTTPS, and signs of injected malware.

Wi-Fi and network

Default router passwords, guest networks that can reach office machines, and devices nobody remembers buying.

Backups

Whether backups exist, whether they're off-site, and whether anyone has ever restored one.

People

A short, friendly phishing test and a 30-minute training session for staff, in English, Spanish or Portuguese.
