# Lockline Security brand book

> Lockline Security is a fictional business, created as a portfolio concept by Jean Berrios-Marquez.

## The brand in one line

**This site should feel calm, precise and trustworthy to small-office owners who know they should "do something about security," unlike enterprise vendors who sell fear and jargon.**

## Who we are

A Worcester security firm for businesses with 5 to 50 people: dental and medical offices, law and accounting firms, restaurants and shops. We find the gaps, explain them in plain English, and help fix them. We serve clients in English, Spanish and Portuguese.

**Personality:** calm, precise, honest, helpful.
**Not:** alarmist, hacker-cool, or vague.

## Voice

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Plain English: "Your front-desk email has no two-factor login." | "Insufficient MFA posture across identity surface" |
| Pair every problem with a fix and a time: "About 1 hour." | List problems without a next step |
| Be honest: "We'll tell you whether a check is worth it for you." | Fear: "Hackers are coming for YOU" |
| Rank by urgency with words: Urgent, Soon, Later | Use color alone to show severity |

**Sample lines**
- Know where your business is exposed before someone else finds it.
- We check all of them without installing anything on your computers.
- Se habla español. Falamos português.

## Logo

- The mark is an amber padlock body with a light shackle, followed by "Lockline" in IBM Plex Sans 700.
- On light backgrounds, use an ink shackle and keep the body amber.
- Don't add glows, circuit lines or shield shapes.

## Color

The full table is in [`design-system/README.md`](design-system/README.md#color).

- **Navy ink** is the base. It's serious without being black.
- **Amber** is the single action color, like a warning light that means "look here."
- **Severity colors** (coral, amber, sky) belong to reports only.
- Rough proportion: 80% navy surfaces, 15% text, 5% amber.

## Typography

- **IBM Plex Sans** everywhere: engineered but friendly.
- **IBM Plex Mono** only for severity tags and report metadata. If it isn't data, it isn't mono.

## Imagery (for the real build)

- Real report screenshots, diagrams of a client's network, and the team at a client's office.
- Avoid hoodies, green code rain, padlocks on circuit boards and glowing globes.

## Signature element

**The sample report.** Showing exactly what a client receives is the whole pitch. Keep it in the hero, keep the findings realistic, and always show the fix.

## Do's and don'ts

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Show prices openly | "Contact us for enterprise pricing" |
| Name the industries you serve | "Solutions for every business" |
| Keep the multilingual line near the contact button | Hide languages in the footer |
| Keep claims checkable (number of checks, days to report) | Promise "100% protection" |
