## Overview

I build websites for small businesses: restaurants, contractors, local offices and shops. Every site is set up securely from launch day.

Most small-business sites look fine on day one and then get hacked a year later through an outdated plugin. I come from cybersecurity (Google Cybersecurity Certificate, studying for Security+), so every site I build launches with:

- A design made for your customers, with one clear action on every page (call, book, buy)
- WordPress or Shopify, built so you can edit it yourself
- SEO basics plus a Google Business Profile that matches your site
- Updates, backups, a firewall and analytics in place before launch

I also contribute to open-source WordPress security plugins (JAT Site Shield and JAT Shell Scanner, both on WordPress.org) and do freelance web development with Jeremy Anderson Tech.

I work in English, Spanish and Portuguese. I'm a U.S. Navy veteran based in Worcester, MA.
